## Implementation boundary

The nominal recipe context now lends a graph that is complete for its exact
closed source profile. The proof borrows the same source function, callable
inventory and checked source call. A private constructor joins their pointer
identities inside the initial-graph loan, then moves that immutable view directly
into a higher-ranked callback. No caller-supplied boolean, cloned owner, row
count or detached completion token can construct it.

This is not a general final capability graph. The ordinary later graph writer
recovers erased GridLeader payload edges; only GridLeaderCurrent can seed that
recovery. The unchanged closed profile refuses that seed and its unsupported
transform families. Completeness therefore applies only to the admitted
profile. Missing/reordered late-edge controls test the independent oracle;
they do not establish positive general GridLeader or enum support.

The wrapper prepays its closure/result/view frame before constructing the
inner callback. The initial factory also accounts for both view frames.
Conservative overlapping frame charges are intentional. A separate 64-unit
work charge precedes the exact source-pointer rejoin. Accepted credits and
partial graph storage remain in the outer owner through error, unwind and
every enclosing postflight. No reset, early refund, mutable gap or replacement
ledger is introduced.

## Next implementation joins

guarded-access vector, checked origins, semantic dereference sites or a ranked
root recipe. Shared index/value preparation is next. Successful guarded-access
appends must then bind to their exact source calls and destinations; volatile
reads and direct writes must not consume guarded ordinals. The shared origin
algorithm remains UNJOINED until authenticated against that actual vector.

effects, assertions, bounds, reference writes, launch binding and ranked
placement in one complete unverified root recipe, followed by mandatory
verification. No launch permission, edited-source promotion, nominal LLVM
continuation, GPU execution or new milestone exit follows from this checkpoint.
