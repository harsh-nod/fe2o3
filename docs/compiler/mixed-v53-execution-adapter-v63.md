# Private V53 Execution Adapter

This is consuming host integration, not production activation. No approved
V53 native-refinement backend is installed. Public recovered and prepared V53
owners remain inert; self-signed receipt fixtures and successful native-content
replay cannot invoke the private execution transition.

The crate-private `ProtectedNativeBackendV53` is an unsafe extension contract.
Its declaration is not evidence that verification exists. A future implementation
must authenticate the exact protected compiler/current-record trust policy and
the admitted V50 proof runtime, original source, complete roster and four graphs.
It receives the same live readmitted V18 owner used by target selection and V60
content replay, the exact current native artifact, and the concrete immutable
prepared request and checked device. The generic evidence representation does
not reuse or relabel an opaque V1 admission or receipt.

The consuming transition binds schema versions V53/V50/V26/V18/COV6, descriptor,
capsule, source and graph identities, exact signed receipt, current compiler
subject/carriage/audit, artifact, selected root, invocation, dispatch contract,
full prepared content and checked device. It checks currentness before and after
verification. Only exact gfx942:xnack- is supported by the existing runtime
authority; gfx950 and other targets are refused, not coerced.

The approved provider must establish source-to-executable semantics and actual
bounds, alias/access, initialization, launch and completion premises. KFD/runtime
inspection is borrowed and allocation-free: it exposes the actual kernarg bytes,
buffers, access/length policy, fixups, geometry and V26 premises without mutable
handles or execution rights. Concurrent-state assumptions must remain secured
through execution by the retained owners. Providers requiring additional guards
must refuse until their custody can be represented; a backend borrow cannot act
as a retained lock or proof session.

The evidence factory checks declared extents before construction and actual
boxed lengths afterwards. It prepays payload and headers on the same ledger;
Vec capacities and conversion coexistence remain the provider's separately
bounded responsibility. Scoped failures and panics restore storage while keeping
work, peak and first denial. The final move-only owner borrows the caller ledger,
holds evidence, current publication and generated output borrows, and releases
its additional retained charge only after drop or runtime completion. The older
preparation charge remains charged by its existing caller-owned convention.
This accounting is not a total provider or host RSS claim.

## Proof Scope

`mixed-default-activation-v28.md` leaves LLVM as a downstream trust boundary.
The existing `WorkerV3SemanticMachineRefinementBackendV1` contract is stronger:
it describes a theorem through final LLVM, ISA and HSACO. The private adapter
does not silently weaken that existing contract or invent a trusted-LLVM policy.
The absence of a full machine theorem is therefore a blocker under the current
host contract, not an inherent requirement of a source-verification task that
explicitly excludes LLVM. A different reviewed deployment policy would still
need exact artifact identity/custody and actual runtime premises. No such approved
provider is supplied here; text correspondence alone is not source-to-LLVM proof.

Tests cover inert composed readmission, versions and subject substitutions,
exact/one-short resources, ledger/floor substitution, error/unwind cleanup and
inspection coordinates. They construct no protected current-record signer,
approved native evidence or checked GPU device. They do not qualify production
authorization, native execution or ordinary default usability.
