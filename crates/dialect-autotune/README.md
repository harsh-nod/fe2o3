# dialect-autotune

`dialect-autotune` is the target-neutral Pliron shell that owns bounded,
inert candidate-set metadata. Its D0 surface records candidate and observation
budgets only; it cannot benchmark, choose a winner, select a compiler, or make
an unadmitted variant legal.

The shell does not lower operations, choose a compiler or hardware target,
produce artifacts, or grant legality, proof, publication, load, tuning, or
launch authority. Its Pliron values and printed syntax are not durable fe2o3
identities.
